Credits

Chart and voice

  • The chart is the vowel chart of the International Phonetic Association.
  • Formant frequencies for the sixteen cardinal vowels are from J. C. Catford, A Practical Introduction to Phonetics, 2nd ed. (2001), p. 154.
  • The synthesizer follows Dennis Klatt, Software for a cascade/parallel formant synthesizer (1980), with the glottal pulse and flutter from Klatt and Klatt, Analysis, synthesis, and perception of voice quality variations among female and male talkers (1990).
  • Voice sizes come from Peterson and Barney, Control methods used in a study of the vowels (1952).

Songs

Every tune is in the public domain. Where the notes were read from a score or a setting someone else prepared, it is linked. The tunes were moved to keys that fit the voice, and the harmonies for three voices were made for this page.

  • Korobeiniki. Russian folk song, to a poem by Nikolay Nekrasov (1861).
  • Blue Danube. Johann Strauss II, op. 314 (1866). Score typeset by Nikos Kouremenos for the Mutopia Project, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Drunken Sailor. Traditional shanty.
  • Can-Can. Jacques Offenbach, from Orpheus in the Underworld (1858).
  • Mountain King. Edvard Grieg, In the Hall of the Mountain King, from Peer Gynt (1875). Score typeset by Coyau for the Mutopia Project, public domain.
  • Skaters' Waltz. Émile Waldteufel, op. 183 (1882). Theme as notated on Wikipedia.
  • Wellerman. New Zealand whaling song. Setting by PacificMist on The Session.
  • Molly Malone. Attributed to James Yorkston (1884). Setting by Alan Parker on The Session.
  • Menuet. J. S. Bach, French Suite no. 3, BWV 814. Score typeset by Knute Snortum and Joel Mayes for the Mutopia Project, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Bella Ciao. Italian folk song. Setting by Henry Jia on The Session.
  • Scarborough Fair. English traditional. Setting by MBAC on The Session.
  • Canon. Johann Pachelbel, Canon in D. Score typeset by Michael Fischer v. Mollard for the Mutopia Project, CC BY 4.0.
  • The Parting Glass. Scots and Irish traditional. Setting by Fiddle Inferno on The Session.
